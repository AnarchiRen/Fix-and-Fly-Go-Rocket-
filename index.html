MISSION BRIEF

Need

Space weather damages real spacecraft. Mission Control needs each failure diagnosed, repaired and reported so future missions survive.

Goals

1. Reach each damaged spacecraft. 2. Diagnose and repair it. 3. Report the findings to Earth.

Mission segments

Launch: your rocket and its parts. Space: your rocket and the damaged spacecraft. Ground: Mission Control, the Earth safe zone. User: the scientists who use your findings.

Requirements

R1 Hull stays above 25%. R2 All 3 spacecraft repaired. R3 All findings transmitted to Earth. R4 At most 1 wrong repair. Each one is checked in your final report.

Process based on NASA’s Design and Develop Science Missions webinar (Systems Engineering Series, March 2025).

Fix and Fly: Go Rocket!

Mission Control sends you to repair three spacecraft damaged by real space weather. Build your rocket, find each one, read its data, repair it, and report to Earth. Collect stars on the way to fund upgrades. Keep hull health above the red safety line.
